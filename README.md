Profile Overview

I am an Agronomist and Data Scientist focused on connecting agricultural research, technology, and evidence-based decision-making. My work combines field knowledge with analytical methods to transform complex datasets into practical insights for more productive and sustainable agricultural systems.

Currently, I am a PhD Candidate in Animal Science at UDESC, where I research integrated crop–livestock systems and multispecies perennial cool-season pastures at NUPEP — the Pasture Ecology and Grazing Management Lab.
